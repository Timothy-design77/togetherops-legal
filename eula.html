Effective September 26, 2026

Who may use TogetherOps

TogetherOps is internal software owned and operated by Together Resorts. Use is limited to Together Resorts and the people it authorizes to manage its books. No license is granted to anyone else, and the software is not distributed to other businesses.

What it does

TogetherOps connects to Together Resorts' own QuickBooks Online company through Intuit's published API to read accounting records so they can be reviewed and reconciled. It is configured to read data; any change to the books is made by an authorized person in QuickBooks, not automatically by TogetherOps.

Authorized use

Authorized users must keep their access private, use TogetherOps only for Together Resorts' accounting work, and follow Intuit's terms of service for QuickBooks Online.

No warranty

TogetherOps is provided as is for internal use. Results support, but do not replace, review by the person responsible for the books.

Disconnecting

The QuickBooks Online administrator can disconnect TogetherOps at any time from QuickBooks (Apps → My apps) or by revoking its access with Intuit. Disconnecting stops all further data access.
