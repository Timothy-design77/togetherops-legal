Privacy policy

Effective September 26, 2026

Data we access

With the QuickBooks Online administrator's authorization, TogetherOps reads accounting data from Together Resorts' own QuickBooks Online company, such as accounts, transactions, vendors, customers, and reports. It does not access any other company's data.

How we use it

The data is used only for Together Resorts' internal bookkeeping review, reconciliation preparation, and follow-up. It is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

Where it is stored

Data and access credentials are kept on a company-controlled computer in the United States under an access-restricted account. Access tokens are stored encrypted and are never published or shared. Only people authorized by Together Resorts can access the data.

Sharing

TogetherOps does not share QuickBooks data with third parties, except with Together Resorts' own accountants or advisers when Together Resorts chooses to, or when required by law.

Retention and removal

Data is kept only as long as needed for Together Resorts' accounting records. When TogetherOps is disconnected from QuickBooks, it stops retrieving data, and its stored credentials are deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the Together Resorts administrator responsible for its books.
